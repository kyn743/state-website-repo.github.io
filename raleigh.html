Raleigh

Where History Meets Innovation

Welcome to Raleigh, the capital of North Carolina. As a lively metropolis and part of the Research Triangle, Raleigh boasts a unique blend of rich history and modern living.

City Highlights

Establishment Year:

1792

Population:

474,000 residents

Average Income:

$75,000 (compared to state average: $72,000)

About The Area

Raleigh is classified as an urban city situated in the Piedmont region of North Carolina.

The central Piedmont region is characterized by its gently rolling hills and rich agricultural land.

As the state capital, Raleigh is a thriving center for education, technology, and governmental activities.
