About The Area

Wilmington is classified as an urban city in the Coastal Plain region of North Carolina.

The Coastal Plain of eastern North Carolina is known for its flat terrain, rich maritime history, and proximity to the Atlantic Ocean.

This location adds to Wilmington's unique coastal character, making it a key destination for tourism and maritime commerce.
